The Eight Major Planets

There are eight official planets in our solar system orbiting the Sun. They are divided into four inner terrestrial (rocky) planets and four outer gas and ice giants.

Note: that all photos shown are link and other photos is downloaded from NASA

1. Mercury

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Terrestrial Planet

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. It has a heavily cratered surface similar to Earth's Moon and extreme temperature changes between day and night.

2. Venus

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Terrestrial Planet

Venus is roughly the same size as Earth. It is surrounded by a thick, toxic atmosphere of carbon dioxide that traps heat, making it the hottest planet in the solar system.

3. Earth

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Terrestrial Planet

Earth is our home planet and the third planet from the Sun. It is the only celestial body known to support life, featuring liquid surface ocean water and a protective atmosphere.

4. Mars

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Terrestrial Planet

Mars is often called the "Red Planet" due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface. It hosts Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system, and thin carbon dioxide atmospheric clouds.

5. Jupiter

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Gas Giant

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, with a mass greater than all other planets combined. It is famous for the Great Red Spot, a massive storm that has raged for centuries.

6. Saturn

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Gas Giant

Saturn is famous for its large, complex ring system made of billions of chunks of ice and rock particles. Like Jupiter, it is made mostly of hydrogen and helium gas.

7. Uranus

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Ice Giant

Uranus is an icy giant planet with a unique blue-green color caused by methane in its atmosphere. It is tilted so far on its axis that it orbits the Sun virtually on its side.

8. Neptune

Photo courtesy of: NASA SCIENCE

Type: Ice Giant

Neptune is the eighth and farthest official planet from the Sun. It is a cold, dark world known for having the fastest supersonic winds recorded anywhere in the solar system.
