Target: LEARN ASTRONOMY

Astronomy is the scientific study of celestial objects, space, and the physical universe as a whole. Derived from the ancient Greek words astron (star) and nomos (law or rule), astronomy literally translates to "the law of the stars."

Our core goal for this website is to break down complex cosmic laws into simple, engaging lessons—helping every student explore the wonders of physical cosmology, planetary geology, and observational science.

Explore Our Solar System

Featured Topic: Gas Giants

Jupiter and Saturn contain over 90% of the planetary mass in our entire solar system. Learn how these massive gravitational anchors shield Earth from incoming asteroids and shape the dynamics of space.

Latest Cosmic Discoveries

Astronomers continue to detect thousands of exoplanets outside our solar system using advanced space telescopes. Discover how scientists look for Earth-like conditions and potential signs of life light-years away.
